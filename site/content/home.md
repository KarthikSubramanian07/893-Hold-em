# 893 Hold'em: co-op poker for 2 to 6

A free cooperative poker game you play in the browser. Your crew sits on one side of a lacquer table in a back-alley bar in Shinjuku's Golden Gai; the House sits across in masks. You win or lose together. You can see the cards of the two players beside you but never your own, and nobody may talk. All you can do is nod at whichever of those two looks strong.

One link, no accounts, no downloads. Open a table, send the link to your friends, and play a night in about fifteen minutes. Empty chairs fill with bot regulars, and you can practice alone first.

## How a hand plays

1. **The deal.** Two cards each, facing out: you see the two players beside you, never yourself. The House holds two cards and shows one.
2. **The nod.** After the turn, each player may nod at one of the two they can see: "you're good". Or pass. The nods turn over together.
3. **In or Out.** After the river, everyone picks In or Out at the same moment.
4. **The showdown.** Every card flips, yours too. Every hand that's In plays the House on its own. Each one that wins moves the marker a step toward the door; each one that loses, a step toward the sirens.

Reach the door for a clean getaway. Reach the sirens and the bar gets raided. After ten hands it's last orders.

## Three tables

The House holds two cards at every table. What changes is how far away the door and the sirens are.

- **Shitamachi**, the corner bar: forgiving, with the sirens a long way off. About seven nights in ten end clean for a crew that plays well.
- **Kabukichō**: neon, noise, and the sirens closer. About one in two.
- **Oyabun**, the boss's room: the sirens close and the door far. About three in ten.
